About Rig Rate Report

An independent guide to what RV and boat storage costs, town by town, using only what facilities publish themselves.

What this site is

Rig Rate Report collects the prices, space types, gate hours, amenities and rules that RV and boat storage facilities post on their own websites, and puts them side by side for one town at a time. Each fact links to the page it came from and shows the date it was checked.

Who writes it

The site is written and maintained under the pen name Glen Hartley. We use a pen name rather than a personal profile; the guide's credibility rests on the source link beside every fact, not on the author.

What this site is not

  • It is not affiliated with any government agency.
  • It is not owned by, affiliated with or paid by any facility it lists, or by any storage marketplace.
  • It does not take reservations or payments. Bookings happen on each facility's own site.
  • It does not publish reviews or star ratings.

How the site may earn money

We may later join affiliate programs. No affiliate links are active yet. See the affiliate disclosure for how any future links will be labeled.
