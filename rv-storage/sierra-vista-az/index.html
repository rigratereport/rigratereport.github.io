Common questions

How much is RV storage in Sierra Vista?

Open spaces post from $40/mo (Sierra Vista Self Storage in Hereford, under 25 ft); AAA Fort Storage's open solar spots start at $60/mo. Covered spaces post at $119.95/mo (12x30) and $144.95/mo (12x40) at U-Haul Sierra Vista Southeast (checked Nov 20, 2026).

From: Sierra Vista Self Storage (Hereford), AAA Fort Storage, U-Haul Moving & Storage of Sierra Vista Southeast (sources on each listing)

Is there a military discount?

AAA Fort Storage lists 15% off for active-duty military, DHS, police and firefighters, and 10% for retired military and those over 55. Pink Door lists 10% off for active duty and first responders and 5% for veterans and seniors 65+ (not combined with move-in promotions).

From: AAA Fort Storage, Pink Door Storage & RV - Sierra Vista (sources on each listing)

Can I dump tanks or charge batteries in storage?

AAA Fort Storage lists a dump station, water and trickle charging for covered RV parking. U-Haul lists its covered spaces with electricity.

From: AAA Fort Storage, U-Haul Moving & Storage of Sierra Vista Southeast (sources on each listing)

Which RV storage facilities in Sierra Vista, AZ post prices online?

3 of the 4 facilities we checked post RV or boat storage prices on their own sites: AAA Fort Storage, U-Haul Moving & Storage of Sierra Vista Southeast, Sierra Vista Self Storage (Hereford). Not posted: Pink Door Storage & RV - Sierra Vista.

Where can I find covered RV or boat storage in Sierra Vista, AZ?

Facilities whose own sites list covered spaces: AAA Fort Storage, U-Haul Moving & Storage of Sierra Vista Southeast. Check each listing for posted sizes and prices.

Where can I find enclosed RV or boat storage in Sierra Vista, AZ?

Facilities whose own sites list enclosed / indoor spaces: AAA Fort Storage, Pink Door Storage & RV - Sierra Vista. Check each listing for posted sizes and prices.

Which Sierra Vista storage lots post 24-hour access?

24-hour access is posted by: Pink Door Storage & RV - Sierra Vista. Other facilities post set gate hours, listed on each facility card.

Can I reserve RV storage online in Sierra Vista?

These facilities link to an online rental, reservation or application page: U-Haul Moving & Storage of Sierra Vista Southeast. Use the "Reserve online" button on each listing.
