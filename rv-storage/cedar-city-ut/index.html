What to know before you book

  • Promotional rates end. 1 facilities show an introductory or first-month offer. Compare the regular monthly rate, which each listing shows where the facility posts it.
  • Availability changes fast. Labels such as "Waiting List", "Only 2 left" or "Coming Soon" appeared on 1 price lines on the check date: EZ Storage.
  • Gate hours not posted for Thunderbird Storage. Ask about access times if you plan early or late departures.
  • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.

Each point above restates facts from the listings on this page; follow the source links on each listing to confirm.

Local context

Cedar City sits on I-15 (exit 57), and the National Park Service's directions to Cedar Breaks National Monument start in town and follow Utah Highway 14 east for about 18 miles, then Highway 148 for 4 miles. Several lots here are on the north side of town near the airport (Aviation Way) and toward Enoch on Highway 91. Airtight says its lot is two minutes from the I-15 off-ramp.

Sources: NPS: Cedar Breaks directions, Airtight Storage, EZ Storage, StorRight. Checked Nov 19, 2026.

Common questions

What does RV storage cost in Cedar City?

Posted outdoor spaces ran from $32/mo (StorRight, 12x20, Enoch) to $70/mo (Airtight, 35-45 ft) on Nov 19, 2026. Covered 40-ft spaces ran $151-$195/mo.

From: StorRight Self Storage (Enoch), Airtight Storage (Cedar City Main Lot), Aviation Storage LLC (sources on each listing)

Is there covered RV storage with power?

StorRight in Enoch posts covered 12x40 RV spaces with power at $161/mo.

From: StorRight Self Storage (Enoch) (sources on each listing)

Which lots have 24-hour access?

EZ Storage, Airtight Storage, Aviation Storage and StorRight all list 24-hour access.

From: EZ Storage, Airtight Storage (Cedar City Main Lot), Aviation Storage LLC, StorRight Self Storage (Enoch) (sources on each listing)

Which RV storage facilities in Cedar City, UT post prices online?

4 of the 5 facilities we checked post RV or boat storage prices on their own sites: EZ Storage, Airtight Storage (Cedar City Main Lot), Aviation Storage LLC, StorRight Self Storage (Enoch). Not posted: Thunderbird Storage.

Where can I find enclosed RV or boat storage in Cedar City, UT?

None of the facilities we checked list enclosed / indoor spaces on their own sites.

Can I reserve RV storage online in Cedar City?

These facilities link to an online rental, reservation or application page: EZ Storage, Airtight Storage (Cedar City Main Lot), Aviation Storage LLC, StorRight Self Storage (Enoch). Use the "Reserve online" button on each listing.
