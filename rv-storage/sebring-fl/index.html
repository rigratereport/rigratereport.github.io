      • Gate hours not posted for Spring Lake Storage, TransVega RV & Truck Center. Ask about access times if you plan early or late departures.
      • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.

      Local context

      Most of the Sebring lots on this page are on or just off US Highway 27, the main north-south road through town: Century Storage and TransVega to the north, and Sebring Self Storage to the south. Spring Lake Storage is on Red Cedar Rd. TransVega also has RV repair and RV and boat washing on site, and it sells quarterly, semi-annual and annual plans, which can suit owners who store for the season.

      Sources: Century Storage Bayview, TransVega RV storage, Sebring Self Storage, Spring Lake Storage. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

      Common questions

      What is the cheapest RV storage in Sebring?

      Best Coast Storage posts 12x30 RV/boat/vehicle parking at $50/mo and 12x60 at $55/mo. Spring Lake Storage posts $50/mo for rigs up to 25 ft (checked Nov 19, 2026).

      From: Best Coast Storage, Spring Lake Storage (sources on each listing)

      Can I pay for a whole season or year up front?

      TransVega RV & Truck Center posts $275 quarterly, $550 semi-annual and $985 annual for RV/camper storage.

      From: TransVega RV & Truck Center (sources on each listing)

      What does storage for a 40-ft motorhome cost?

      Spring Lake Storage posts $75/mo + tax for up to 42 ft. Century Storage lists a 12x40 open paved space at $123/mo online, but it was listed as sold out on Nov 20, 2026.

      From: Spring Lake Storage, Century Storage - Bayview (sources on each listing)

      Which RV storage facilities in Sebring, FL post prices online?

      4 of the 5 facilities we checked post RV or boat storage prices on their own sites: Best Coast Storage, Spring Lake Storage, Century Storage - Bayview, TransVega RV & Truck Center. Not posted: Sebring Self Storage.

      Where can I find covered RV or boat storage in Sebring, FL?

      None of the facilities we checked list covered spaces on their own sites.

      Where can I find enclosed RV or boat storage in Sebring, FL?

      Facilities whose own sites list enclosed / indoor spaces: Sebring Self Storage. Check each listing for posted sizes and prices.

      Which Sebring storage lots post 24-hour access?

      None of the facilities we checked post 24-hour access; each listing shows the posted gate hours or says they are not posted.

      Can I reserve RV storage online in Sebring?
