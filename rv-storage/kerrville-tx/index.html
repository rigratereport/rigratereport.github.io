RV & Boat Storage in Kerrville, TX

8 facilities · 72 sourced facts · last checked Nov 18, 2026 · by Glen Hartley

At a glance

  • Open RV/boat parking posts from $29/mo (12x35, first 3 months, then $54/mo) at Mount Storage; Another Closet posts a 15x30 parking space at $62/mo (web price). (Mount Storage Kerrville, Another Closet Storage - Kerrville; see sources below)
  • Mount Storage says its parking spaces are open-air; its enclosed 10x20 to 10x30 vehicle units post from $94/mo for the first 3 months ($119/mo after). (Mount Storage Kerrville; see sources below)
  • Enclosed RV-size units are offered by Junction Hwy (up to 14x47, with power for a trickle charger) and, in Center Point, Self Storage Near Me (15x45 and 15x50); neither posts prices on its own site. (Junction Hwy RV and Self Storage, Self Storage Near Me - Center Point; see sources below)
  • Posted access hours: 5 AM-11 PM (Mount Storage), 6 AM-11 PM (Safe & Secure's three lots), 6 AM-10 PM (Another Closet), 8 AM-6 PM (Junction Hwy). (Mount Storage Kerrville, Safe & Secure Storage of Kerrville - South, Another Closet Storage - Kerrville, Junction Hwy RV and Self Storage; see sources below)
  • E-Z Self Storage, Junction Hwy, Safe & Secure and Self Storage Near Me do not post prices; call or reserve online for rates. (E-Z Self Storage (Central, Thompson Dr), Junction Hwy RV and Self Storage, Safe & Secure Storage of Kerrville - South, Self Storage Near Me - Center Point; see sources below)
