RV & Boat Storage in Casa Grande, AZ

4 facilities · 41 sourced facts · last checked Nov 18, 2026 · by Glen Hartley

At a glance

  • Open stalls post from $55/mo (20x12) to $100/mo (50x12) at Arizona Allard; CG RV Storage posts uncovered 30-45 ft at $89-$119/mo including taxes. (Arizona Allard RV Boat & Trailer Storage, CG RV Storage; see sources below)
  • Covered spaces post from $140/mo (30x12 with power, plus $15 set-up) at Airpark and $159-$179/mo (30-45 ft, taxes included) at CG RV Storage. (Airpark Boat & RV Storage, CG RV Storage; see sources below)
  • Park 'N Shade does not post prices on its own site. (Park 'N Shade RV - Casa Grande; see sources below)
  • Posted access hours range from 24 hours (Arizona Allard) to 5 AM-10 PM (Airpark), 6 AM-9 PM (CG) and 7 AM-7 PM (Park 'N Shade). (Arizona Allard RV Boat & Trailer Storage, Airpark Boat & RV Storage, CG RV Storage, Park 'N Shade RV - Casa Grande; see sources below)
  • Free dump station, fresh-water fill and tire air for renters are posted by Airpark; Park 'N Shade posts dump stations. (Airpark Boat & RV Storage, Park 'N Shade RV - Casa Grande; see sources below)
