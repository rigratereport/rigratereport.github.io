What to know before you book

  • One-time and extra fees. Posted here: Airpark Boat & RV Storage: $15 one-time set-up fee.
  • Promotional rates end. 1 facilities show an introductory or first-month offer. Compare the regular monthly rate, which each listing shows where the facility posts it.
  • Availability changes fast. Labels such as "Waiting List", "Only 2 left" or "Coming Soon" appeared on 2 price lines on the check date: Airpark Boat & RV Storage, Arizona Allard RV Boat & Trailer Storage.
  • Conflicting prices on one site. Arizona Allard RV Boat & Trailer Storage: Home page headline says 'Starting at $59.00'; the size list shows $55. Ask which rate applies before you sign.
  • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.
