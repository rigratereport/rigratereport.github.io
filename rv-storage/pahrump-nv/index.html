    What to know before you book

    • One-time and extra fees. Posted here: AZ Storage & RV Parking: Site says all prices are shown with no hidden fees or admin charges.
    • Promotional rates end. 3 facilities show an introductory or first-month offer. Compare the regular monthly rate, which each listing shows where the facility posts it.
    • Gate hours not posted for Highway 160 Self Storage. Ask about access times if you plan early or late departures.
    • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.

    Each point above restates facts from the listings on this page; follow the source links on each listing to confirm.

    Local context

    Pahrump is a desert town in southern Nye County along Nevada State Route 160, and several lots here describe themselves by nearby landmarks: Calvada Meadows Airport, Simkins Park, and Lakeside Casino and RV Park on Homestead Road. Covered RV space is scarce. AZ Storage & RV Parking, which opened in June 2025, told the Pahrump Valley Times it has 48 RV spaces, 10 of them covered. Its covered spots were sold out when we checked.

    Sources: Highway 160 Self Storage, Lakeside Storage, Pahrump Valley Times: AZ Storage and RV Parking grand opening, AZ Storage & RV Parking. Checked Nov 19, 2026.

    In the facilities' own words

    Common questions

    How much is RV storage in Pahrump?

    Posted monthly prices on Nov 19, 2026 ran from $50 (Lakeside Storage outside parking, call for availability) to $129.99 (Value Plus, 30x10). Storage Kings posts a 40x10 RV spot at $75.

    From: Lakeside Storage, Value Plus Storage, Pahrump Storage Kings (sources on each listing)

    Is covered RV parking available in Pahrump?

    AZ Storage & RV Parking posts covered 13x45 spaces at $250/mo, but they were sold out (waitlist) when we checked. Highway 160 Self Storage says it has covered vehicle parking but did not show prices.

    From: AZ Storage & RV Parking, Highway 160 Self Storage (sources on each listing)

    Which Pahrump lots allow 24-hour access?

    Pahrump Storage Kings and Value Plus Storage both list 24-hour access.

    From: Pahrump Storage Kings, Value Plus Storage (sources on each listing)

    Which RV storage facilities in Pahrump, NV post prices online?

    4 of the 6 facilities we checked post RV or boat storage prices on their own sites: AZ Storage & RV Parking, Pahrump Storage Kings, Value Plus Storage, Lakeside Storage. Not posted: Highway 160 Self Storage, Price Is Right Mini & RV Storage.

    Where can I find enclosed RV or boat storage in Pahrump, NV?

    None of the facilities we checked list enclosed / indoor spaces on their own sites.

    Can I reserve RV storage online in Pahrump?
