What to know before you book

  • Promotional rates end. 2 facilities show an introductory or first-month offer. Compare the regular monthly rate, which each listing shows where the facility posts it.
  • Availability changes fast. Labels such as "Waiting List", "Only 2 left" or "Coming Soon" appeared on 1 price lines on the check date: Russell Lane Storage (Hometown Storage).
  • Gate hours not posted for Russell Lane Storage (Hometown Storage), Hometown Storage - Gassville, Twin Gables RV and Boat Storage, Mountain Home Dry Storage. Ask about access times if you plan early or late departures.
  • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.

Each point above restates facts from the listings on this page; follow the source links on each listing to confirm.

Local context

Mountain Home is a lake town in the Arkansas Ozarks. The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers describes Norfork Lake, near Mountain Home, as 22,000 surface acres with a 380-mile shoreline and 19 Corps parks with boat ramps; most of those parks are open April/May through October. That seasonal rhythm is why covered stalls and tall enclosed units are the main product here. Twin Gables keeps its office in Mountain Home and its storage lot in Gamaliel.

Sources: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers: Norfork Lake, Twin Gables: Find us. Checked Nov 20, 2026.

Common questions

How much is covered boat or RV storage in Mountain Home?

Covered 40-ft stalls post at $100/mo (Russell Lane Storage) and $125/mo (TakeCoverMH; Hometown Storage's Gassville lot), checked Nov 19, 2026.

From: Russell Lane Storage (Hometown Storage), TakeCoverMH, Hometown Storage - Gassville (sources on each listing)

Can I rent a fully enclosed unit big enough for an RV?

Yes. Mountain Home Dry Storage lists enclosed units up to 50x15x15 ($300/mo), and Twin Gables lists 15x45 and 15x50 units with 14-ft doors from $220-$265/mo. 62 Bypass has 14x40 bays but does not post prices.

From: Mountain Home Dry Storage, Twin Gables RV and Boat Storage, 62 Bypass RV & Mini Storage (sources on each listing)

Is there power for a battery charger?

TakeCoverMH has electrical outlets for charging batteries only. Twin Gables and some Mountain Home Dry Storage units list electricity.

From: TakeCoverMH, Twin Gables RV and Boat Storage, Mountain Home Dry Storage (sources on each listing)

Which RV storage facilities in Mountain Home, AR post prices online?

5 of the 6 facilities we checked post RV or boat storage prices on their own sites: TakeCoverMH, Russell Lane Storage (Hometown Storage), Hometown Storage - Gassville, Twin Gables RV and Boat Storage, Mountain Home Dry Storage. Not posted: 62 Bypass RV & Mini Storage.

Which Mountain Home storage lots post 24-hour access?

24-hour access is posted by: TakeCoverMH, 62 Bypass RV & Mini Storage. Other facilities post set gate hours, listed on each facility card.

Can I reserve RV storage online in Mountain Home?

These facilities link to an online rental, reservation or application page: TakeCoverMH, Russell Lane Storage (Hometown Storage). Use the "Reserve online" button on each listing.
