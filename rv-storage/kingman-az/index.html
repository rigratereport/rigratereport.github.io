What to know before you book

  • One-time and extra fees. Posted here: Mohave Storage - 3528 N Pinal St: $10 set-up fee.
  • Promotional rates end. 1 facilities show an introductory or first-month offer. Compare the regular monthly rate, which each listing shows where the facility posts it.
  • Availability changes fast. Labels such as "Waiting List", "Only 2 left" or "Coming Soon" appeared on 2 price lines on the check date: High Desert Boat and RV Storage, Mohave Storage - 3528 N Pinal St.
  • Insurance and paperwork. Big Box Storage: Registration and proof of insurance required; month to month; facility says it does not raise your rate while you rent.
  • Gate hours not posted for Mohave Storage - 3528 N Pinal St, Kingman Marine, Blake Ranch RV Park (storage). Ask about access times if you plan early or late departures.
  • Measure your rig. Big Box Storage: 40' spaces fit many Class A motorhomes per the facility; largest luxury coaches may not fit.
  • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.

Each point above restates facts from the listings on this page; follow the source links on each listing to confirm.
