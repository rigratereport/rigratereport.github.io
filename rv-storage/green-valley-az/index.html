What to know before you book

  • Promotional rates end. 1 facilities show an introductory or first-month offer. Compare the regular monthly rate, which each listing shows where the facility posts it.
  • Availability changes fast. Labels such as "Waiting List", "Only 2 left" or "Coming Soon" appeared on 1 price lines on the check date: Green Valley RV & Self Storage (Green Valley Covered RV).
  • Gate hours not posted for Santa Cruz Valley Boat & RV Storage, Continental Self Storage. Ask about access times if you plan early or late departures.
  • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.

Each point above restates facts from the listings on this page; follow the source links on each listing to confirm.

Common questions

How much is RV storage in Green Valley?

Santa Cruz Valley Boat & RV Storage posts 12-ft-wide spots from $59.99/mo (15 ft) to $199.99/mo (50 ft). Green Valley RV & Self Storage posts uncovered 12x30 at $137/mo regular ($68.50 for the first 3 months), checked Nov 20, 2026.

From: Santa Cruz Valley Boat & RV Storage, Green Valley RV & Self Storage (Green Valley Covered RV) (sources on each listing)

Is covered RV storage available?

Green Valley RV & Self Storage posts covered 10x35 to 12x40 spaces ($204-$229/mo regular), but all were waitlist-only when we checked.

From: Green Valley RV & Self Storage (Green Valley Covered RV) (sources on each listing)

What are the access hours?

Green Valley RV & Self Storage lists access daily 6 AM-10 PM.

From: Green Valley RV & Self Storage (Green Valley Covered RV) (sources on each listing)

Which RV storage facilities in Green Valley, AZ post prices online?

2 of the 3 facilities we checked post RV or boat storage prices on their own sites: Green Valley RV & Self Storage (Green Valley Covered RV), Santa Cruz Valley Boat & RV Storage. Not posted: Continental Self Storage.

Where can I find enclosed RV or boat storage in Green Valley, AZ?

None of the facilities we checked list enclosed / indoor spaces on their own sites.

Can I reserve RV storage online in Green Valley?

These facilities link to an online rental, reservation or application page: Green Valley RV & Self Storage (Green Valley Covered RV), Santa Cruz Valley Boat & RV Storage. Use the "Reserve online" button on each listing.
