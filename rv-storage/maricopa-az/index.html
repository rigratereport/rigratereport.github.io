What to know before you book

  • Promotional rates end. 1 facilities show an introductory or first-month offer. Compare the regular monthly rate, which each listing shows where the facility posts it.
  • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.
