    What to know before you book

    • One-time and extra fees. Posted here: Show Low RV & Mini Storage (Show Low Auto RV & Boat Storage): All contracts are charged 8.1% sales tax.
    • Insurance and paperwork. Spartan RV Storage: 6-month minimum (6- or 12-month terms, then month-to-month); RVs and boats must be year 2000 or newer; registration, driver's license and proof of full-coverage insurance required; prices are plus tax.
    • Gate hours not posted for Spartan RV Storage. Ask about access times if you plan early or late departures.
    • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.

    Each point above restates facts from the listings on this page; follow the source links on each listing to confirm.

    Local context

    Show Low sits in the White Mountains, and two storage operators here describe themselves as close to Show Low Lake, Fool Hollow Lake and the towns of Pinetop-Lakeside, Snowflake and Taylor. If you plan to launch at Fool Hollow Lake Recreation Area, Arizona State Parks currently says there is no boat access at the park; only vessels that can be hand-launched are allowed. One lot here sets its gate hours by season, so check access times before a winter pickup.

    Sources: Arizona State Parks: Fool Hollow Lake Recreation Area, US Storage Centers Show Low page, Spartan RV Storage, Show Low RV & Mini Storage. Checked Nov 19, 2026.

    Common questions

    What is the cheapest posted RV storage in Show Low?

    Spartan RV Storage posts a 15x12 outdoor space at $39/mo on a 12-month term, and Burton RV Storage posts an 11x15 space from $40/mo (checked Nov 19, 2026). Both are uncovered.

    From: Spartan RV Storage, Burton RV Storage (sources on each listing)

    Is there covered RV storage in Show Low?

    US Storage Centers on N. Auto Mall Parkway says it has covered RV and boat spaces up to 75 ft. Spartan and Burton say they are outdoor only. The Timber Mesa and Show Low RV & Mini Storage pages don't say their spaces are covered.

    From: US Storage Centers - Show Low, Spartan RV Storage, Burton RV Storage (sources on each listing)

    Can I get to my RV at any hour?

    Burton RV Storage and Timber Mesa list 24-hour access. US Storage Centers lists 6 AM-9 PM, and Show Low RV & Mini Storage lists 8 AM-5 PM from Sept 16 to May 15.
