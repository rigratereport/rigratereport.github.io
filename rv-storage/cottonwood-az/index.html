What to know before you book

  • One-time and extra fees. Posted here: Cottonwood Springs RV Park & Storage (formerly Air Park Storage): Sales tax not included; 3% credit card fee.
  • Promotional rates end. 4 facilities show an introductory or first-month offer. Compare the regular monthly rate, which each listing shows where the facility posts it.
  • Availability changes fast. Labels such as "Waiting List", "Only 2 left" or "Coming Soon" appeared on 1 price lines on the check date: Alpha Self Storage - South 7th Street.
  • Gate hours not posted for Columbia Self Storage - North Alamos Drive. Ask about access times if you plan early or late departures.
  • Get the total in writing. Ask for the monthly rate, any admin or setup fee, deposit, tax and lock requirement before you pay. Facilities on this page set and change their own prices.

Each point above restates facts from the listings on this page; follow the source links on each listing to confirm.

Local context

Cottonwood sits in Arizona's Verde Valley, near downtown shops and wineries, and Cottonwood Springs says its park has views of the Sedona red rocks. Boulder Creek, a few miles away in Camp Verde, pitches its covered sites to RV owners from Sedona, Phoenix and Flagstaff, and to Canadians storing long-term. Covered sites are what fill up here: most of Columbia's covered sizes had waitlists when we checked.

Sources: Cottonwood Springs RV Park, Boulder Creek RV Storage, Columbia Self Storage Cottonwood. Checked Nov 19, 2026.

Common questions

How much is covered RV storage in Cottonwood?

Cottonwood Springs' price sheet lists covered 12-ft sites from $145/mo (35 ft) to $270/mo (70 ft). Columbia posts a covered 12x45 at $220/mo (web rate). Boulder Creek in Camp Verde posts covered sites from $150/mo (12x30).

From: Cottonwood Springs RV Park & Storage (formerly Air Park Storage), Columbia Self Storage - North Alamos Drive, Boulder Creek RV Storage (Camp Verde) (sources on each listing)

Is there an RV dump station?

Boulder Creek RV Storage in Camp Verde lists a dump station and propane refilling.

From: Boulder Creek RV Storage (Camp Verde) (sources on each listing)

Are there pull-through sites?

Yes. Cottonwood Springs lists pull-through prices for most sizes from 50 ft (uncovered) and 35 ft (covered). Boulder Creek says to ask about pull-through sites.

From: Cottonwood Springs RV Park & Storage (formerly Air Park Storage), Boulder Creek RV Storage (Camp Verde) (sources on each listing)

Which RV storage facilities in Cottonwood, AZ post prices online?

4 of the 4 facilities we checked post RV or boat storage prices on their own sites: Cottonwood Springs RV Park & Storage (formerly Air Park Storage), Alpha Self Storage - South 7th Street, Columbia Self Storage - North Alamos Drive, Boulder Creek RV Storage (Camp Verde).

Where can I find enclosed RV or boat storage in Cottonwood, AZ?

Facilities whose own sites list enclosed / indoor spaces: Cottonwood Springs RV Park & Storage (formerly Air Park Storage). Check each listing for posted sizes and prices.

Which Cottonwood storage lots post 24-hour access?

24-hour access is posted by: Boulder Creek RV Storage (Camp Verde). Other facilities post set gate hours, listed on each facility card.

Can I reserve RV storage online in Cottonwood?
