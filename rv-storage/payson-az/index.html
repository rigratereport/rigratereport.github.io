  • Availability changes fast. Labels such as "Waiting List", "Only 2 left" or "Coming Soon" appeared on 1 price lines on the check date: Patriot RV and Boat Storage.

Local context

Payson-area lots sit on Highway 87 and Highway 260, and the RV-only lots are south of town toward Rye. Rye Creek Storage says it is 11 miles south of Payson on Highway 87, and Patriot RV and Boat Storage says it is near Tonto National Forest and Roosevelt Lake. Both RV-only lots post prices on their own sites, but Patriot's spaces were waitlist-only and Rye Creek asks you to call for availability.

Sources: Rye Creek Storage, Patriot RV and Boat Storage, Rim View RV & Boat Storage. Checked Nov 21, 2026.

Common questions

What does RV storage cost in Payson?

Patriot RV and Boat Storage posts 30x12 and 40x12 spaces at $49/mo (waitlist). Rye Creek Storage posts $50/mo under 25 ft and $60/mo over 25 ft (checked Nov 21, 2026).

From: Patriot RV and Boat Storage, Rye Creek Storage (sources on each listing)

Is there an RV dump station?

Rye Creek Storage lists an RV dump station, clean well water and a compressed air station.

From: Rye Creek Storage (sources on each listing)

Is covered RV storage available?

None of the open Payson-area lots we checked says its spaces are covered. Rim View RV & Boat Storage plans covered spaces but was still under construction on Nov 21, 2026.

From: Rye Creek Storage, Patriot RV and Boat Storage, Star Valley Storage (U-Haul dealer) (sources on each listing)

Which RV storage facilities in Payson, AZ post prices online?

3 of the 3 facilities we checked post RV or boat storage prices on their own sites: Rye Creek Storage, Patriot RV and Boat Storage, Star Valley Storage (U-Haul dealer).

Where can I find enclosed RV or boat storage in Payson, AZ?

None of the facilities we checked list enclosed / indoor spaces on their own sites.

Which Payson storage lots post 24-hour access?

24-hour access is posted by: Patriot RV and Boat Storage, Star Valley Storage (U-Haul dealer). Other facilities post set gate hours, listed on each facility card.

Can I reserve RV storage online in Payson?

These facilities link to an online rental, reservation or application page: Rye Creek Storage, Patriot RV and Boat Storage, Star Valley Storage (U-Haul dealer). Use the "Reserve online" button on each listing.
