How we check facts

Rig Rate Report is written and maintained by Glen Hartley (a pen name). It is an independent guide: not owned by, affiliated with or paid by any storage facility, listing marketplace or government agency.

Rules every listing follows

  1. Primary source only. Every price, hour, amenity, rule and security feature is copied from the facility's own website or its own online rental page (or, where stated, the facility's named leasing agent). We do not copy figures from other directories or from AI tools.
  2. One fact, one source link, one date. Each fact shows the exact page it came from and the date we checked it. Each facility card shows its most recent check as "Last verified".
  3. No estimates. If a facility does not post a price, address or gate hours, the listing says "not posted" rather than a guess or an average.
  4. Conflicts are shown, not resolved. When a facility's own pages disagree, we show both and note the conflict.
  5. Promotions are labeled. First-month discounts and online-only rates are marked as such, next to the regular rate when one is posted.
  6. Availability is a snapshot. Labels such as "Waiting List" or "Only 2 left" are copied as shown on the check date and change quickly.
  7. Location notes are the facility's words. The "Local context" section quotes how facilities describe their own location (for example, distance to an interstate). We do not measure distances ourselves.
  8. Derived labels are read from the text. The size and space-type columns and badges are read from the facility's own wording; if the text does not say, we show "Not stated".
  9. Buttons go to the facility. Website, Reserve online and Call buttons use links and numbers published by the facility. Directions open a Google Maps search for the published street address.
  10. No reviews, no ratings, no paid placement. Facilities cannot pay to be listed or ranked.
  11. Monthly recheck. Every fact older than 30 days is rechecked against its source; facts that can no longer be confirmed are removed.

Corrections

Facility owners and readers can report a correction on our public issue tracker. We update a fact only after confirming it on the facility's own page.
