How we check facts

Rig Rate Report is written and maintained by Glen Hartley (a pen name). The site is independent and is not owned by, or affiliated with, any storage facility or listing marketplace.

Rules every listing follows

  1. Primary source only. Every price, hour, amenity and security feature is copied from the facility's own website or its own online rental page (or, where stated, the facility's named leasing agent). We do not copy figures from other directories or from AI tools.
  2. One fact, one source link, one date. Each fact shows the exact page it came from and the date we checked it.
  3. No estimates. If a facility does not post a price, the listing says "No prices posted" rather than a guess or an average.
  4. Conflicts are shown, not resolved. When a facility's own pages disagree (for example a home-page "starting at" figure that differs from its unit list), we show both and note the conflict.
  5. Promotions are labeled. First-month discounts and online-only rates are marked as such, next to the regular rate when one is posted.
  6. Availability is a snapshot. Labels such as "Waiting List" or "Only 2 left" are copied as shown on the check date and change quickly.
  7. No reviews, no ratings, no paid placement. We do not publish star ratings or reviews, and facilities cannot pay to be listed or ranked.
  8. Monthly recheck. Every fact older than 30 days is rechecked against its source; facts that can no longer be confirmed are removed.

Corrections

Facility owners and readers can request a correction. We will update a fact only after confirming it on the facility's own page.

Rig Rate Report lists rates exactly as each facility posts them on its own website, with the source link and check date beside every fact. Prices change; confirm with the facility before you rent. Facility owners: corrections are welcome; see How we check.
